**WATER QUALITY RULES 2027**

++**A TEAM OF UP TO: 2++ 			++APPROXIMATE TIME: 30 minutes**++

  


++**DESCRIPTION++:**

Participants will be assessed on their understanding and evaluation of freshwater aquatic environments.

  


++**EVENT PARAMETERS:**++

This test will occur online and consist of thirty questions. External materials will not be allowed. 

  


++**THE COMPETITION**:++ 

This may include analysis, interpretation or use of charts, graphs and sample data. Process skills may include collecting and interpreting online data, measuring, analyzing data, and making inferences. The test will cover the following three topics:                                                       

  


1. **Freshwater Ecology** 

This section will use multiple choice questions in areas such as: aquatic ecology, water cycle, nutrient cycling, aquatic chemistry and its implications for life, potable water treatment, waste water treatment, aquatic food chains/webs, community interactions, population dynamics, sedimentation pollution, and harmful species.

  


1. **Freshwater Macroinvertebrates**

This section will require participants to identify (common name only) adult macroinvertebrates and aquatic nuisance organisms listed below and to understand their importance as indicators of water quality.

1. **Water Monitoring and Analysis**

Participants should be able to understand and interpret data related to testing procedures and purposes for collecting data related to **salinity, pH, phosphates, turbidity, dissolved oxygen, temperature, nitrates, biochemical oxygen demand, and their relationship to one another**.

  


++**SCORING:**++

1. Highest score wins.
2. Selected questions will be used to break ties.
3. Time that the test was submitted may be taken into account as a further tiebreaker.
