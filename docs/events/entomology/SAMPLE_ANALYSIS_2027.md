## Patterns in the supplied tests

From the **35 keyed items** (not from invented stems):

1. **Heavy identification** — family and order ID dominate Sample Test 1 (Papilionidae, Corixidae, Curculionidae, Cynipidae, Coccinellidae, Trichoptera, Tipulidae, Ixodidae, Ephemeroptera, mixed-rank sets).
2. **Image specimens** — most ID items are written as if a picture is shown. Images were not in the restated text.
3. **Five-option (A–E) ID** — Test 1 keys include **E** (Q3, Q9, Q11, Q24, Q26, Q28), so at least some items have five choices. Jr. Explorer Astronomy uses four (`a`–`d`). Product design should allow four or five later; do not change app types in this docs task.
4. **Paired look-alikes** — Corixidae vs Notonectidae; Hydrophilidae vs Dytiscidae.
5. **Anatomy vocabulary** — body regions, chitin/exoskeleton, thoracic segments, cerci, tarsi, leg anatomy, fossorial and natatorial legs, Megaloptera mouthparts.
6. **Life cycle** — complete metamorphosis (Test 1 Q10).
7. **Correlated / applied** — mayfly habitat, Reduviidae feeding, Cynipidae galls.
8. **Non-insect** — Ixodidae; Insecta vs Ixodidae.
9. **Behavior** — stridulation (one item).
10. **Classification mix** — one item mixes Diplura (order) with families (Test 1 Q15).

Approximate mix of the 34 items with known topics: ~20 identification, ~8 anatomy/adaptation, ~4 ecology/behavior/application, ~2 classification/life cycle. (Q30 unknown.)

## Claims to verify before production questions

Do not change the sample keys. Flag for authors:

1. Any diagnostic taken from a missing **image**.
2. **Chitin** / exoskeleton wording (Sample Q5; Test 1 Q5).
3. **Fossorial** and **natatorial** definitions (Sample Q4; Test 1 Q24).
4. **Stridulation** mechanism and which listed taxon the sample used (Test 1 Q6).
5. **Corixidae vs Notonectidae** and **Hydrophilidae vs Dytiscidae** characters (Test 1 Q8, Q14, Q19).
6. **Reduviidae feeding** (Test 1 Q22).
7. **Mayfly habitat** (Test 1 Q21).
8. **Cynipidae galls** (Test 1 Q27).
9. **Termites** vs list wording **cockroaches/termites** under Blattodea (Test 1 Q28).
10. **Complete metamorphosis** stages (Test 1 Q10).
11. **A–E lettering** for slash-separated taxon lists (Test 1 Q1, Q15).
12. Test 1 **Q30** stem unknown; only key **C** is supplied.
