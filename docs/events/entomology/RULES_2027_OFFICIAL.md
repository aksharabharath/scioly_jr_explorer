**ENTOMOLOGY RULES 2027**

++**DESCRIPTION**++: Students will be asked to identify insects by indicated taxonomy (order, subclass, or family), answer questions about insects, and use or construct a dichotomous key. All specimens will be representatives of insects found in the Contiguous United States. 

++**A TEAM OF UP TO:**++ 2 

++**APPROXIMATE TIME:**++ 30 minutes

++**1. EVENT PARAMETERS:**++

a. Each team can have one (1) cheat sheet, containing information in any form and from any source. Sheet protectors, lamination, tabs, and labels are allowed.

b. In addition to their cheat sheet, each team may have one (1) copy of the ESO Entomology List provided.

c. This test will be conducted online in a multiple choice format.

++**2. THE COMPETITION:**++

1. Teams will be asked to identify an insect's Order, Subclass, Family or common name and answer a related question(s). Questions are limited to topics below and insects are limited to those listed on the 2027 ESO Entomology List.
2. Insect images will be exhibited so that students will be able to see pertinent features. Students may be asked to use or formulate a simple dichotomous key to identify insects.
3. For each specimen, students will be asked correlated questions that pertain to the insect’s internal and external anatomy, ecology, economic characteristics, or systematics.
4. Ecological characteristics may include habitats, adaptations to the environment, behavior, relationships (e.g. symbiosis and competition) with animals, plants, and public health, as well as climate change impacts.
5. Economic characteristics may include beneficial or detrimental aspects of insects such as sources of food, medicine, disease, chemicals, nutrients, and insects as nuisance species.

++**3. SCORING**++: 

a. The high score wins. 

b. Preselected questions will be used as tiebreakers.
