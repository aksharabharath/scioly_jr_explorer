## Event description

**Official (supplied) — competition purpose as stated in the 2027 rules**

Students **will** be asked to identify insects by indicated taxonomy (order, subclass, or family), answer questions about insects, and use or construct a dichotomous key. All specimens will be representatives of insects found in the Contiguous United States. The attached list also includes Class Entognatha and Non-Insect Arthropods (Family Ixodidae); that heading vs “insects” is unresolved in the rules text.

Competition adds: identify Order, Subclass, Family **or** common name; questions **limited to** topics below; insects **limited to** the 2027 ESO Entomology List; images **will** be exhibited; students **may** be asked to use or formulate a **simple** dichotomous key.

Do not flatten **may** / **e.g.** / **such as** into required checklists. See domains below.

## Competition Scope

**Official (supplied)**

1. Identification by **order**
2. Identification by **subclass** where applicable (Collembola is the subclass named on the list)
3. Identification by **family**
4. Identification by **common name** (as printed on the ESO list)
5. **Correlated questions** attached to a specimen or taxon (not identification alone)
6. **Image-based** specimen questions (insect images **will** be exhibited so students can see **pertinent features**)
7. Dichotomous keys are **in scope**. Description: students **will** be asked to **use or construct** a dichotomous key. Competition: students **may** be asked to **use or formulate** a **simple** dichotomous key. Do not treat keys as a numeric quota; do not ignore the **may** vs **will** wording.
8. Online **multiple-choice** items

Do not add genus/species identification as an official requirement unless a later official packet says so.

## Required Knowledge Domains

**Organizational** grouping. Domain names and examples below are **official (supplied)** unless marked otherwise.

### Identification

**Official (supplied):** Identify listed taxa by order, subclass where applicable, family, and common name, including from images.

### Taxonomy / Systematics

**Official (supplied):** Follow the ESO Entomology List hierarchy (Class → Subclass → Order → Family). See `TAXON_LIST_2027.md`. Correlated questions **may** pertain to **systematics** (with anatomy, ecology, or economic characteristics).

### External Anatomy

**Official (supplied):** External anatomy is in scope. Sample tests also treat body regions, exoskeleton/chitin, mouthparts, legs (including fossorial and natatorial), tarsi, and cerci — those details are **sample content**, not an official required-structure checklist.

### Internal Anatomy

**Official (supplied):** Internal anatomy is in scope.

**Organizational:** No official organ-by-organ checklist was restated. Do not invent one.

### Ecology

**Official (supplied):** Correlated questions **will** pertain to **ecology**. Ecological characteristics **may include** habitats, adaptations to the environment, behavior, relationships (**e.g.** symbiosis and competition) with animals, plants, and public health, as well as climate change impacts.

**Organizational:** `may include` and `e.g.` are **not** a closed fact catalog and **not** `must know` checklists. Adaptations, behavior, relationships, public health, and climate-change impacts sit in this official sentence; they are not separate closed official domain lists.

### Economic characteristics

**Official (supplied):** Correlated questions **will** pertain to **economic characteristics**. Those **may include** beneficial or detrimental aspects of insects **such as** sources of food, medicine, disease, chemicals, nutrients, and insects as nuisance species.

**Organizational:** `may include` / `such as` are examples, not an exhaustive economic catalog.

### Life cycles

**Official (supplied):** Life cycles / metamorphosis are **not named** in the 2027 rules body. Do not treat them as an official required domain.

### Asterisks on the list

**Official (supplied):** Fourteen list lines are marked `*`. The rules **do not define** the marker. See `TAXON_LIST_2027.md`.
