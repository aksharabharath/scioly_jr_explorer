**CRIME BUSTERS RULES 2027**

  


++**DESCRIPTION:**++ Given a scenario, a collection of evidence, and possible suspects, students will perform a series of tests. Test results along with other evidence will be used to solve a crime and answer questions.

  


++**NUMBER OF PARTICIPANTS:**++ 2			++**APPROXIMATE TIME:**++ 45 minutes

  
  


++**SAFETY**:++ Each participant must wear their own pair of safety goggles (covering the sides of the eyes), and a lab coat or apron during the competition. Face masks and gloves are recommended but not required. **Participants are responsible for bringing the aforementioned supplies to the competition themselves.**

  


**Safety Precautions:**

1. Wear appropriate personal protective equipment  
2. Avoid breathing in mist, vapors, or spray 
3. Participants must wear safety goggles, an apron or lab coat, and have skin covered from the neck down to the wrist and toes. Participants removing safety clothing or unsafely handling materials/equipment will be penalized/disqualified as determined by the judge.
  1. Gloves are optional but if the host requires a specific type, teams will be notified
  2. Shoulder length or longer hair must be tied back.

  


++**EVENT PARAMETERS:**++

1. “Cheat Sheet”: Each team may bring one 8.5” x 11” sheet of paper that may contain information on both sides in any form and from any source without any external materials attached to the paper.
2. Each participant may bring a stand-alone non-programmable, non-graphing calculator.
3. The Supervisor will provide:
  1. Iodine
  2. HCl (hydrochloric acid)
  3. Chromatography pen
  4. Wash bottle with distilled water (no more than 250 mL)
  5. Waste container(s)
4. For materials participants are recommended to bring, see Recommended Materials.

  


++**THE COMPETITION:**++

1. **Fingerprint Analysis**
  1. Identify and compare fingerprints from the crime scene with those of the suspects.
  2. Identify the parts (minutiae) of a fingerprint.
  3. Identify the parts of the skin.
  4. Identify fundamental patterns of fingerprints and its specifics:
    1. Loops (Ulnar, Radial, Central Pocket)
    2. Whorls (Plain, Accidental, Double Loop)
    3. Arches (Plain, Tented)
2. **Hair/Fiber Analysis**
  1. Identify the differences between human, dog, cat, squirrel, and mouse hair samples in microscopic images.
  2. Identify the differences between animal, vegetable, and synthetic fibers in microscopic images.
    1. Participants will be asked to distinguish between cotton, linen, silk, nylon, rayon, and polyester.
3. **Soil Analysis**
  1. Identify the following soil types using provided samples:
    1. Peaty
    2. Loamy
    3. Sandy
    4. Clay
    5. Chalky
    6. Silty
4. **Chemical Analysis**
  1. Identify various unique powders and mixtures.
    1. Participants will be asked to identify the uses and the chemical formulas of every powder. 
    2. Strictly iodine, HCl, and distilled water will be provided to perform tests.
  2. The following powders may be tested: 
    1. Table Salt
    2. Sugar
    3. Flour
    4. Cornstarch
    5. Baking Soda
    6. Yeast
    7. Calcium carbonate (Chalk)
    8. Gelatin
  3. Participants should also be able to identify the following liquids (and their chemical formulas) using their pH  (participants will need their own pH strips to test the liquids):
    1. Lemon juice
    2. Rubbing alcohol (isopropyl)
    3. Hydrogen peroxide (3%)
    4. Liquid bleach
    5. Vinegar
    6. Water
  4. Participants should be prepared to receive 3-5 powders and 2-4 liquids to identify on the test.
  5. ++**NOTE:++ Please do not consume the powders/liquids used in this event for testing.**
5. **Overall Analysis:** Write a paragraph of analysis
  1. Format: Name the suspect who committed the crime, their motive, and name all pieces of evidence and why each one incriminates the suspect.
6. **Material Disposal:** Teams should dispose of waste as directed by the event supervisor.

  


++**NOTE:++** This event will have questions that will relate to a crime scene story as well as theoretical questions that test the participants’ overall knowledge about the topics given.    
  
++**SCORING:**++

1. The team with the highest score wins. Time of completion will not be accounted for in the scoring process.
  1. Fingerprint analysis: 20%
  2. Hair/Fiber Analysis: 15%
  3. Soil Analysis: 20%
  4. Chemical Analysis: 25%
  5. Overall Analysis: 20%
2. Penalty Rule: 5%
  1. 5% may be deducted from the total score if the area is not cleaned up as instructed by the Event Supervisor, if a team brings prohibited lab equipment, or does not bring proper safety equipment to the event. If participants adhere to the rules, no points will be deducted. This penalty strictly deducts points and does not provide any benefits or additions to the score.
3. Tiebreakers: For tiebreakers, a specific question set will be predetermined.

  


++**MATERIALS: (to bring to competition)**++

1. Safety goggles and lab coat/ apron - students **WILL NOT** be allowed to handle Hydrochloric acid (supplied at the event) without all of these items.
2. Cheat Sheet - 1 double sided 8.5” x 11” sheet of paper - only one sheet allowed per team
3. Scientific calculator (non-programmable, non-graphing)
4. 3 to 5 Droppers/pipettes
5. 5 pH strips
6. 5 Small Spoons to scoop powder
7. Spot plates - 15 to 20 wells

  


**First aid instructions** 

1. If hydrochloric acid is swallowed, rinse the mouth and seek medical advice
2. If hydrochloric acid is on the skin, remove contaminated clothing and rinse the skin with water for at least 15 minutes
3. If hydrochloric acid is in the eyes, rinse eyes with water for at least 15 minutes and seek medical advice
4. If hydrochloric acid is inhaled, move to fresh air and seek medical advice
5. **WARNING: Hydrochloric acid is corrosive and can cause severe burns to the skin, eyes, and digestive tract. It can also irritate the respiratory tract. Be very cautious in experimentation.**
