## 0. How to read this inventory

### Authority

Only statements in `RULES_2027.md` are treated as official for this pass.

If a field is absent from that file, it is recorded as **`NOT STATED`**. Gaps are not filled from general knowledge.

### Evidence labels (scope vs fact)

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `RULES-SCOPE` | The rules say this topic, skill, list, or format **can or will be tested**. Does **not** by itself verify a scientific answer. |
| `SOURCE-VERIFIED` | The answer text is fully supplied by the rules file (a closed list, heading, or exact logistics sentence). |
| `SOURCE-NEEDED` | In scope, but the rules do not supply the factual detail needed to verify a question. |
| `IMAGE-SOURCE-NEEDED` | Visual or physical-sample interpretation is in scope, but this repo has no usable asset. |
| `HUMAN-REVIEW` | Official wording is ambiguous, incomplete, or combinable in more than one reasonable way. |

A row may carry both a **scope** label and a **fact** label.

### Open vs closed wording

| Rules wording | How this inventory treats it |
|---|---|
| Numbered named subtypes (Loops / Whorls / Arches; named soils; named powders; named liquids; named fibers) | Closed for the **printed names**. Do not add siblings. |
| **The following powders may be tested** | Closed **candidate** set of eight names. `may` means a given contest need not use all eight. Do **not** add unlisted powders. |
| “Identify the parts (minutiae) of a fingerprint” / “parts of the skin” | Skill in scope. **No** part names are listed. |
| Chromatography pen (provided) | Named as a supplied item. Chromatography is **not** a numbered competition section. |

| Property | Record |

Iodine; HCl (hydrochloric acid); Chromatography pen; Wash bottle with distilled water (**no more than 250 mL**); Waste container(s).

## 7. Safety (in-file text; not a science answer key)

PPE: own goggles (covering sides of eyes), lab coat or apron; face masks and gloves recommended not required; participants bring supplies.

Safety Precautions: PPE; avoid breathing mist/vapors/spray; goggles, apron or lab coat, skin covered neck to wrist and toes; penalty/DQ for removing safety clothing or unsafe handling; gloves optional unless host specifies type; shoulder-length or longer hair tied back.

HCl: students **WILL NOT** be allowed to handle it without goggles and lab coat/apron.

First aid: swallow / skin / eyes / inhale instructions; **WARNING:** HCl corrosive, severe burns, respiratory irritation.

Do not consume powders/liquids used for testing.
