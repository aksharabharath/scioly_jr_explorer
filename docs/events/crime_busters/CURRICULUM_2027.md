## 0. How to read this document

### Scope vs factual evidence

The rules establish **what can be tested**. They do **not** automatically establish formulas, pH values, microscope diagnostics, or test outcomes.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `RULES-SCOPE` | Named as testable content, format, or skill. |
| `SOURCE-VERIFIED` | The rules themselves contain the answer (printed name, heading, logistics sentence). |
| `SOURCE-NEEDED` | Students are expected to know or do this, but the fact is not in the rules. |
| `IMAGE-SOURCE-NEEDED` | Needs prints, micrographs, soil photos/samples, or a lab stimulus this repo does not have. |
| `HUMAN-REVIEW` | Official text is ambiguous; do not “fix” it in a future bank without a decision. |

### Knowledge vs skill

- **Knowledge:** a named list item, heading, or required topic (e.g. the six soil names).
- **Skill:** what the student **does** (compare prints; identify a powder with allowed reagents; write the analysis paragraph).

Do not invent extra skills from first-aid or materials lists.

Event logistics and safety bound the tournament; they are §1, not a science topic.
