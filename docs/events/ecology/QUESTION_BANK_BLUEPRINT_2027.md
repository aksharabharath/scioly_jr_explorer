## 3. Slot types that **are** in MVP (still unwritten)

### 3.2 Levels of organization (~3)

| Allowed operation | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Recall the **printed** six-name sequence Individual → … → Biosphere | V-ORG (T1-RULES) |
| Match population / community / ecosystem / biome / biosphere to the quoted definition | V-F6–F10 |
| Distinction: community is multiple species; population is one species in an area | V-F6, V-F7 |

**Not in MVP:** treating OpenStax’s four research levels as the official list (V-F11, HR8); diagrams of the hierarchy (no asset).

### 3.3 Energy, pyramids, nutrient cycling (~3)

| Allowed operation | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Food webs show directional energy flow | V-F12 |
| Pyramids of numbers or biomass **may** be inverted; energy pyramids are **always** upright because energy is lost as heat | V-F15–V-F17 |
| Biogeochemical cycle = recycling of inorganic matter between organisms and environment | V-F18 |

**Not in MVP:** the **10% rule** as a number (SN1); TLTE formula or Silver Springs 14.8% as a contest fact (V-F13 note); pyramid **figures** as stems (IM4); a closed catalog of which cycles exist.

### 3.4 Succession and community interactions (~4)

| Allowed operation | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Primary vs secondary succession (new land vs remnants remain) | V-F19 |
| Climax community as a stable equilibrium until the next disturbance | V-F20 |
| Predation vs herbivory | V-F21 |
| Intraspecific = same species | V-F22 |
| Competitive exclusion (same niche / all the same resources) | V-F23 |
| Mutualism / commensalism / parasitism under a **broad** symbiosis definition | V-F24, HR9 |

**Not in MVP:** seral stages (SN5); grouping “types” vs climax (HR4) as a keyed distinction; interspecific definition until SN23 has a quote; lynx–hare cycle numbers or figures.

### 3.5 Population dynamics (~3–4)

| Allowed operation | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Carrying capacity *K* = maximum population an environment can support | V-F26 |
| Exponential needs unlimited resources (model); logistic levels off at *K* | V-F27 **qualitative only** |
| Density-dependent vs density-independent (biotic examples vs weather/disasters/pollution) | V-F28, V-F29 |
| Uniform / random / clumped dispersion **names** | V-F30 |
| *r*- vs *K*-selected trait contrast as quoted | V-F32, HR10 |

**Not in MVP:** doubling-time formula (SN2); overshoot/dieback (SN3); logistic/exponential **equations** (SN24); life-table or survivorship **figures** (IM2, IM5). Type I/II/III **text** is optional (V-F31) and is **not** an official printed list — cap at **1** if used, or skip.

### 3.6 Selection, speciation, gene flow (~3–4)

| Allowed operation | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Stabilizing vs directional as quoted | V-F34, V-F35 |
| Sexual selection / sexual dimorphism as quoted | V-F37 |
| Three adaptation **category names** (morphological, physiological, behavioral) | V-ADP, V-F38 |
| Allopatric vs sympatric as quoted | V-F39 |
| Adaptive radiation as quoted | V-F40 |
| Gene flow = movement of alleles; genetic drift as chance allele-frequency change | V-F41, V-F42 |

**Not in MVP:** defining **disruptive** from OpenStax **diversifying** (HR11) — name-recognition of the four official type **names** is allowed without that definition; frequency-dependent selection (SN27); convergent vs divergent (SN6); which five mass-extinction **events** (SN7). “There have been five mass extinctions” (V-F43) is at most **one** optional item — do not name the events.

### 3.7 Climate and biome distribution (~2)

| Allowed operation | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Coriolis: deflection right (NH) / left (SH) due to Earth’s rotation | V-F44 |
| Rain shadow: more precipitation windward, much less leeward | V-F45 |

**Not in MVP:** Hadley cells / biome-belt mechanism (SN12); climate **maps** (IM3).

### 3.8 Deserts, grasslands, named biome examples (~4)

Bold heading is **Ecology of Deserts and Grasslands**. Prefer North American grassland/desert facts that are quoted.

| Allowed operation | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Temperate grasslands / prairies in central North America; grasses dominate | V-F50 |
| Subtropical deserts very dry; evaporation can exceed precipitation | V-F51 |
| Desert plants: tiny leaves or no leaves to reduce water loss (ocotillo after rain) | V-F52 |
| Arctic tundra north of boreal forest (location as quoted) | V-F48 |
| Tropical wet forest = tropical rainforest, equatorial (as an **example** biome, not “featured NA”) | V-F46 |

**Not in MVP:** OpenStax’s eight-biome catalog as official (HR1, SN28); treating **taiga** and **boreal forest** as two different biomes (HR2) — if both strings appear, do not key a false distinction; **savannah** spelling vs OpenStax savanna location outside North America (HR3, HR1); a desert/grassland **type** catalog (SN10); extra adaptations beyond V-F52 (SN11).

### 3.9 Biodiversity and ecosystem services (~3)

| Allowed operation | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Biodiversity considers number of species and relative abundance | V-F53 |
| Genetic diversity vs ecosystem diversity as quoted | V-F54, V-F55 |
| Four official service **names** (Provisioning, Regulating, Supporting, Cultural) as a closed name set | V-ES |
| Ecosystem services include clean air/water, fertile soil, pollination, flood control (**examples**, not mapped to the four headings) | V-F56, V-F57, HR12 |

**Not in MVP:** which example is Provisioning vs Regulating (SN8).

### 3.10 Climate change and habitat (~2–3)

| Allowed operation | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Greenhouse gases trap heat; EPA main gases CO2, CH4, N2O, fluorinated gases | V-F58 |
| Ocean: absorbs CO2 → more H+ / lower pH; less carbonate for shell-builders | V-F60 **qualitative** |
| Habitat loss, overharvesting, and exotic species as major proximate threats | V-F61, V-F63 |
| Four habitat-process **names** as printed (deforestation, urbanization, wetland drainage, agricultural expansion) | T1-RULES / RS16 — **names only** |

**Not in MVP:** mixing EPA vs OpenStax GHG lists in one keyed answer (HR13); NOAA 0.1 pH / “30% increase in acidity” unless a human explicitly wants those figures; mechanisms of each habitat process (SN13).

### 3.11 Invasives and overexploitation (~2–3)

| Allowed operation | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Invasive = nonnative + threatens ecosystem balance | V-F25 |
| Four characteristic **names**; four control-method **names** | V-INV-C, V-INV-M |
| Biological / chemical / cultural / mechanical as quoted by NISIC | V-F62, HR14 |

**Not in MVP:** NISIC physical/manual as a fifth official method (HR14); invented species lists (SN16); diet/tolerance/predator biology beyond the names (SN15); pathogen vectoring / habitat alteration mechanisms (SN17).

### 3.12 Acid deposition, biomagnification, eutrophication (~3)

| Allowed operation | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Acid rain/deposition: acidic precipitation or dust; SO2 and NOx → sulfuric/nitric acids | V-F64 |
| Biomagnification: persistent toxins increase up trophic levels | V-F65 |
| Eutrophication: nutrient runoff → excess microbes → depleted oxygen → fauna die | V-F66 |
| N and P as major limiting aquatic nutrients; oligotrophic / mesotrophic / eutrophic | V-F67 |

**Not in MVP:** eutrophication **stage** ladders (SN18); a sharp bioaccumulation-only definition beyond V-F65 (SN19); soil erosion / desertification / salinization facts (SN14).

### 3.13 Alternative energy (~2–3)

Cover **several** of the six official types, not all six as separate flashcards.

| Allowed operation | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Six type **names** as the closed energy set | V-EN |
| Solar: no GHGs **when operating**; manufacturing/land effects exist | V-F69 |
| Nuclear: no CO2 **while operating**; radioactive waste; no U.S. permanent high-level dump | V-F70 |
| Wind: no routine air/water pollution; bird/bat collision risk | V-F72 |
| Hydro: no direct air pollutants; dams affect fish migration and river conditions | V-F73 |
| Biomass: combustion releases CO2; wood smoke; harvest faster than growth → deforestation | V-F71 |
| Geothermal: does not burn fuel; much lower emissions than fossil (EIA’s own comparison) | V-F74 |

Prefer **one pro and one con** that are both in the same quotation. **Not in MVP:** capacity factors, g/kWh, mixing EIA percent claims with other inventories.

### 3.14 IUCN and conservation (~2)

| Allowed operation | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Nine Red List **category names**; VU, EN, CR = threatened with extinction | V-F75 |
| Reintroduction and captive breeding as named conservation practices (qualitative OpenStax) | V-F76, V-F77 |

**Not in MVP:** IUCN criteria A–E or numeric thresholds (SN25); a category **table** image (IM6); reclamation vs reintroduction (SN20); goals of conservation biology as a closed list (SN21); Yellowstone elk/wolf as required natural-history detail (optional color in an explanation only, not a keyed fact unless kept strictly to V-F76’s reintroduction sentence).
