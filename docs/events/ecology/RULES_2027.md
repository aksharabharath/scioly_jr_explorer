**ECOLOGY RULES 2027**

++**A TEAM OF UP TO: 2++ 		++APPROXIMATE TIME: 30 minutes**++

# ++**DESCRIPTION:**++

Students will answer questions involving content knowledge and process skills in the area of ecology and adaptations in featured North American biomes. 

# ++**EVENT PARAMETERS:**++

This test will occur online and consist of thirty questions. External materials will not be allowed. 

# ++**THE COMPETITION:**++ 

The event will emphasize these process skills as they apply to ecology: defining variables; analyzing data from graphs and tables; forming hypotheses; making predictions.

- General Principles of Ecology
  - Food webs and trophic pyramids, nutrient cycling, community interactions
    - Levels of ecological organization: Individual → Population → Community → Ecosystem → Biome → Biosphere
    - Rules of energy and nutrient transfer in ecosystems (e.g., 10% rule, Pyramids of number, biomass, and energy, Biogeochemical cycles)
    - Types of ecological succession (Primary v.s. Secondary, Climax communities, Seral stages)
    - Community Interactions: Symbiosis, Competition (Intraspecific vs. Interspecific, etc.), Predation and herbivory dynamics
  - Population dynamics
    - Carrying capacity (*K*): Overshoot, Dieback, etc.
    - Limiting factors: Density-dependent factors  vs. Density-independent factors
    - Population distribution & Demographics: Dispersion patterns, Life tables, Survivorship curves, Life history strategies (*r*-selected vs. *K*-selected)
    - Growth models (Logistic, Exponential, Doubling time)
  - Extinction, selection, and migration. 
    - Immigration and Emigration: Gene flow, Open vs. closed populations
    - Natural selection: Types of selection (Directional, stabilizing, disruptive, sexual), Adaptations (Morphological, physiological, behavioral)
    - Speciation (Allopatric vs. Sympatric)
    - Biodiversity and evolution: Adaptive radiation, Convergent vs. divergent evolution, Mass extinction events, Genetic Drift
- Terrestrial Ecosystems
  - Climate and Biome Distribution (Coriolis effect, Rain shadow effect, etc.)
  - Major terrestrial biomes (ex. Rainforests, taiga, tundra, desert, boreal forest, savannah, e.t.c)
  - **Ecology of Deserts and Grasslands** (e.g., Desert/Grassland types, Adaptations of plants and animals)
  - Understand basic concepts of biodiversity (e.g., importance, different types)
  - Importance and Ecosystem Services (Provisioning, Regulating, Supporting, Cultural)
- Human Impact on Ecosystems
  - Climate change (e.g., Greenhouse gases, Ocean acidification)
  - Habitat destruction & Fragmentation (Deforestation, Urbanization, Wetland drainage, Agricultural expansion)
  - Invasive species: Characteristics (Life history strategy, diet, tolerance, predator presence), Impact mechanisms (e.g., Competitive exclusion, habitat alteration, pathogen vectoring), Control methods (Biological, Chemical, Mechanical, Cultural) 
  - Overexploitation such as overfishing, hunting, and resource depletion
  - Acid deposition (including acid rain) and chemical contamination (pollution)
    - Biomagnification
    - Bioaccumulation
    - Eutrophication (e.g., Stages, Causes)
  - Soil erosion: Water/wind erosion, desertification, loss of topsoil, salinization 
  - The pros and cons of using alternative energy and its effect on the environment
    - Solar, hydroelectric, wind, geothermal, biomass, and nuclear energy
  - Understand the goals of conservation biology and the IUCN Red List for conservation status
    - Conservationist strategies
    - Habitat restoration
    - Wildlife preservation
    - Captive breeding programs
    - Endangered and threatened species
    - Reclamation vs. Reintroduction

  
**SCORING:**

1. Highest score wins.
2. Selected questions will be used to break ties.
3. Time that the test was submitted may be taken into account as a further tiebreaker.
