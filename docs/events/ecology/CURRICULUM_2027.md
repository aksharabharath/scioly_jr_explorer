## 0. How to read this document

### Scope vs factual evidence

The rules establish **what can be tested**. They do **not** automatically establish the factual answer to a science question.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `RULES-SCOPE` | Named as testable content, format, or skill. |
| `SOURCE-VERIFIED` | The rules themselves contain the answer (printed name, sequence, logistics sentence). |
| `SOURCE-NEEDED` | Students are expected to know or do this, but the fact is not in the rules. |
| `IMAGE-SOURCE-NEEDED` | Needs a graph, table, map, or other asset this repo does not have. |
| `HUMAN-REVIEW` | Official text is ambiguous or oddly spelled; do not “fix” it in a bank without a decision. |

Do **not** mark a biological or environmental fact `SOURCE-VERIFIED` merely because the rules list it as a heading, an `e.g.` example, or something students should “understand.”

### Knowledge vs skill

- **Knowledge:** a printed name, sequence, or named topic students may need to recognize or know.
- **Skill:** something the rules say students must **do**.

The four process skills are **skills**. Nested outline nouns are **knowledge-scope names**, not extra process skills.

### Hierarchy

| Level | Count | What is counted |
|---|---|---|
