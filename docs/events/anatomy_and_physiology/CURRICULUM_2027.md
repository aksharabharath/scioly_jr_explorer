## 0. How to read this document

### Evidence labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `RULES-SUPPORTED` | The rules explicitly state the concept, named entity, skill, limit, or requirement. |
| `LIST-SUPPORTED` | The name appears on the appended **2027 ESO Major Skeletal Muscles List**. |
| `SOURCE NEEDED` | Students must know something, but the rules do not provide enough factual detail to verify a specific question. |

Requirement and fact are separate. Example:

- Biceps brachii origin / insertion / function **must be known** → `RULES-SUPPORTED` + `LIST-SUPPORTED`
- “Biceps brachii originates from …” → `SOURCE NEEDED`
- PTH affects the skeletal system → `RULES-SUPPORTED`
- Detailed molecular mechanism of PTH → `SOURCE NEEDED`

### Knowledge vs skill

- **Knowledge:** a named fact, entity, category, or required topic (names of listed muscles; named diseases; named hormones).
- **Skill:** what the student **does** (label bone surface anatomy on imaging; distinguish osteoarthritis from rheumatoid arthritis; determine origin/insertion/function of a listed muscle).

Only skills directly supported by the rules are listed. Diagram-based **muscle** identification is **not** listed as a skill: diagrams are explicit for bone surface anatomy, not for muscles.

### “e.g.” versus “limited to”

- **limited to / as listed:** closed list. Do not add siblings.
- **e.g.:** named examples only. Not an unlimited topic expansion.

## 9. Ambiguities and source gaps

1. **2025 vs 2027 muscle-list title.** Competition items say “2025 National Major Skeletal Muscles List.” The appended list is “2027 ESO Major Skeletal Muscles List.” This curriculum uses the appended 28 names only.
2. **Muscle count is 28, not 30.**
3. **List printing:** `Extensor carpi radialis` is not split into longus/brevis; `Iliopsoas` is one name; other quadriceps/hamstrings are not added.
4. **“e.g.” is not a closed catalog** (functions, features, allergens, joint types, hormones, vertebrae, tension, contraction, origin/insertion/function).
5. **Osteomalacia/rickets** slash-joining is unexplained.
6. **No official bone list, skin-layer names, joint-type catalog, origin/insertion table, treatment table, Salter-Harris grades, or burn-classification scheme** in the rules.
7. **RANKL** is named; bone/marrow/cartilage cell names are not.
8. **Integumentary treatments/prevention** are required; muscular-disease treatments are not restated.
9. **Division, question count, tiebreak identity, and scoring weights** are not stated.
10. Almost no question can be fact-verified from the rules file alone. Scope can be verified; biology cannot.
