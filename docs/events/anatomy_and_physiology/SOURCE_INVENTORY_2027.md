## 0. How to read this inventory

### Authority

Only statements in `RULES_2027.md` are treated as official for this pass.

### Evidence labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `RULES-SUPPORTED` | The rules explicitly state this concept, named entity, skill, limit, or requirement. |
| `LIST-SUPPORTED` | The name appears on the appended **2027 ESO Major Skeletal Muscles List**. |
| `SOURCE NEEDED` | The rules require students to know something, but do **not** supply the underlying factual details needed to verify a specific question. |

A row may carry both a requirement label and a fact label. Example:

- Requirement: students must know biceps brachii origin / insertion / function → `RULES-SUPPORTED` + `LIST-SUPPORTED`
- Fact: the actual origin/insertion text → `SOURCE NEEDED`

### “e.g.” versus “limited to”

| Rules wording | How this inventory treats it |
|---|---|
| **limited to** / **as listed** | Closed official list. Do not add siblings. |
| **e.g.** | Named examples. In scope as named. **Not** permission to invent an unlimited related set. |
| **etc.** | Signals that the named examples are not a complete catalog. Do **not** invent the missing items. |

## 6. Ambiguities and source gaps

1. **Muscle-list title mismatch.** Competition text cites the **2025 National Major Skeletal Muscles List**. The appended list is the **2027 ESO Major Skeletal Muscles List**. This inventory uses the appended 28 names and does not import any other year’s list.
2. **Muscle count is 28, not 30.** No extra names were added to reach 30.
3. **List printing details.** `Extensor carpi radialis` has no longus/brevis split. `Iliopsoas` is one printed name. Incomplete quadriceps/hamstring sets are **not** completed from general knowledge.
4. **“e.g.” lists are examples.** Functions, dermatological features, allergens, joint types, hormones, vertebral types, tension topics, and contraction topics are not closed catalogs.
5. **“limited to” / “as listed” lists are closed.** Infections, inflammatory skin disorders, skin cancers, skeletal disease list, strains/sprains, and muscular disease groups must not be expanded.
6. **Allergens vs infections.** Poison ivy and metals are allergen **examples**, not in the “limited to” infection list.
7. **Osteomalacia/rickets** is slash-joined; the rules do not say whether to treat them as one or two conditions.
8. **No bone list, no skin-layer names, no joint-type catalog, no origin/insertion table, no treatment table, no Salter-Harris grade table, no burn-classification table** appear in the rules.
9. **RANKL** is named; bone cell types are not.
10. **Integumentary treatments/prevention** are required; muscular-disease treatments are not explicitly restated.
11. **Division, question count, and tiebreak identity** are not stated.
