# Entomology practice images

Practice uses **photographs from Wikimedia Commons**, not generated drawings and not official Science Olympiad figures. Quiz files are JPEGs. Some add a yellow circle or A/B letters only. They do **not** print family names, common names, or the correct choice.

Student-facing `imageAlt` text follows the same rule.

`*-photo.jpg` files are the unmodified Commons downloads used to build the quiz JPEGs.

## Credits

Licenses and authors below were checked against Wikimedia Commons file-page metadata. Quiz JPEGs (circles, A/B composites, re-encodes) are **adaptations**. CC BY and CC BY-SA still require attribution. The practice quiz shows those credits under the photo (`imageCredit` on live questions). Public-domain and CC0 photos do not require a student credit line.
