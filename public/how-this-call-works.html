What you still need

The app part is ready. A real ring still needs two phone numbers.

You type: the number that should ring (your phone).

A grown-up sets: a Vapi number to call FROM. If the Vapi account has none, add one in the Vapi dashboard, then put VAPI_PHONE_NUMBER_ID in the hidden file named .env.local.

The secret Vapi key is already in that hidden file. It is not on this page. To open this picture later, go to /how-this-call-works.html while the app is running, or click “How the call works” after you log in.
